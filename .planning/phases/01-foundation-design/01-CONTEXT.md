<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Color & Visual Identity
- Primary background: Dusty pink/salmon — warm, unexpected, like stepping into a sunset
- Text/borders: Black for primary text, colored darks (purple/navy) for accents and shadows
- Accent color: Teal/green for buttons, links, highlights — cool complement to warm pink (reference: green plane against pink sky in example images)
- Neobrutalist intensity: Bold & chunky — thick borders (4-6px), heavy offset shadows, unmistakably brutalist
- Reference images: WS_Parachute-Ending stills capture the vibe — cosmic, warm, distinctive

### Typography System
- Headings: Quirky display font with personality — something distinctive that stands out
- Body text: Claude's discretion — prioritize readability for blog posts while maintaining good contrast with quirky headings
- Scale: Claude's discretion — appropriate hierarchy for the design
- Font selection: Claude researches and recommends fonts fitting neobrutalist + cosmic vibe

### Home Page Layout
- Hero focus: Bold name/title front and center
- Content: Just name — maximum impact, minimal text
- Position: Centered with breathing room — prominent but with visible space around
- Below hero: Nothing — minimal home page, navigation takes visitors elsewhere

### Navigation Design
- Desktop: Fixed header, always visible at top when scrolling
- Desktop nav styling: Claude's discretion
- Mobile: Bottom nav bar — fixed icons at bottom of screen, thumb-friendly
- Social links (GitHub, LinkedIn): Footer only — keeps main nav clean
- Footer relationship: Social links in scrollable footer, nav bar stays fixed below

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact typography scale and body font choice (prioritize readability)
- Desktop nav item styling (text links vs buttons)
- Loading states and transitions
- Responsive breakpoint specifics
- Footer design details

</decisions>
